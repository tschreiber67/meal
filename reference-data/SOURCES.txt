OFFLINE SOURCE DATA
Retrieved October 24, 2026.

This folder contains reference inputs, not an implemented importer. The current
application reads only data/foods.json and data/recipes.json. Those remain
fictional examples. NUTRITION_REQUIREMENTS.txt still defines the next bounded
backend change using its existing fixtures. These sources do not expand that
change or alter its expected values.

NUTRITION AND PORTION WEIGHTS

USDA FoodData Central, Agricultural Research Service.
https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/

nutrition/foundation-subset.json: 66 food records, 79 portion entries.
Release: April 2026. Root array: FoundationFoods.
nutrition/sr-legacy-subset.json: 143 food records, 377 portion entries.
Release: April 2018, final SR Legacy release. Root array: SRLegacyFoods.
nutrition/fndds-subset.json: 46 food records, 220 portion entries.
FNDDS 2021-2023, released October 2024. Root array: SurveyFoods.
nutrition/food-index.json: searchable names, source sets, IDs, portion counts.

The 255 records include multiple preparations of some foods, not 255 unique
ingredients. Source food objects are retained intact. Exact IDs and original
archive hashes are in source-manifest.json. These are selected common foods,
not exhaustive coverage of every ingredient in every recipe. The Foundation
archive contains 32 null entries; these are excluded, not converted to foods.

foodNutrients preserves nutrient IDs, names, units, amounts and source metadata.
foodPortions preserves measure descriptions and gram weights where available.
Not every food has a portion entry or every nutrient. Household portion weights
are reference measurements, not recommended dietary serving sizes.
Read documentation/fdc-download-field-descriptions.pdf for field definitions.

Keep raw/cooked, drained/undrained, added salt and nutrient units distinct.
Multiple energy measures and nutrient definitions can occur in the source.
No nutrient-selection policy, ingredient crosswalk, missing-value imputation,
or raw-to-cooked conversion is implemented by this source bundle.

RECIPES

USDA, Recipes for Healthy Kids: Cookbook for Homes.
https://www.fna.usda.gov/tn/recipes-healthy-kids-cookbook-homes

recipes/ contains eight ASCII text excerpts and an index with original PDF
URLs, source-file hashes and page numbers. Each excerpt contains ingredients,
directions, preparation/cooking times, yield and published nutrients per
serving. Original quantities and recipe wording are retained; fractional
characters are written as 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, etc. Recipe quantities were checked
against the rendered original pages. School stories, photos and food-group
credit sections are omitted. This keeps the archive small and focused.

Chic' Penne
Mediterranean Quinoa Salad
Oodles of Noodles
Rainbow Rice
Sweet Potato and Black Bean Stew
Lentils of the Southwest
Confetti Soup
Spanish Chickpea Stew

All eight source recipes yield six servings, but a serving's volume varies.
The recipe texts are reference documents, not the current app's recipe schema.
Source nutrition figures are published estimates, not new calculations or
acceptance-test expected values. They must not replace the mock fixture goldens.
The current app's eight recipes remain separately labeled illustrative recipes.

PRICES

USDA Economic Research Service, Fruit and Vegetable Prices.
https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/fruit-and-vegetable-prices

prices/ers-fruits-2023.csv: 62 product/form rows.
prices/ers-vegetables-2023.csv: 93 product/form rows.
These are 2023 estimates, posted December 9, 2025. Original CSV bytes retained,
including UTF-8 BOM, units and source preparation-yield factors. Retail values
use the source's pound/pint units; edible-cup prices have their own columns.
These are national estimates, not local store quotes. ERS says these tables
are not suitable for comparing price changes across years.

US Bureau of Labor Statistics, Average Price Data, food series.
https://download.bls.gov/pub/time.series/ap/

prices/bls-food-monthly-2024-2026.tsv: 6,396 monthly observations across
343 area/item series. January 2024 through September 2026 where published.
Areas: U.S. city average, Northeast, Midwest, South and West.
Some series have gaps or ended earlier. No values have been filled in.
M13 annual averages are excluded. These are prices, not CPI index values.
Join series_id to bls-series.tsv for the item, area and unit description.
bls-item.tsv, bls-area.tsv and bls-footnote.tsv are original lookup files.
documentation/bls-format.txt is the original BLS file-format description.
Observation and series TSV fields have surrounding whitespace removed.

USDA Economic Research Service, Purchase to Plate National Average Prices
from the Purchase to Plate Suite (2023 data product).
https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/purchase-to-plate

prices/ppnap-2017-2018.csv: all 4,435 records in the latest public cycle,
2017/2018. Values are nominal dollars per 100 edible grams, as consumed.
This is historical broad coverage, not current retail package pricing.
CSV was transcoded from Windows-1252 to UTF-8 without changing field values.
documentation/ppnap-source-readme.txt explains source fields and methodology.
Only public National Average Prices data is included. Restricted crosswalk,
price-tool and ingredient-tool datasets are not included.

Food codes are source identifiers. FNDDS and PP-NAP refer to different release
years; matching names or codes is not proof of a valid cross-release match.
The bundle supplies no food-to-price crosswalk and no inflation adjustment.

Price estimates differ in year, geography and purchase/edible basis. Retain
these distinctions. No package-size rounding, yield conversion, pantry logic,
budget optimizer or automatic recipe conversion is included here.

PROVENANCE AND REUSE

source-manifest.json records source URLs, download dates, hashes, selected
IDs, transformations and bundled paths. checksums.sha256 records local file
integrity. THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt identifies attribution and reuse statements.
No API key, OAuth account, npm package or internet access is needed to read
these bundled files. Original bulk archives and recipe photo PDFs are omitted
to reduce upload size; selected source records and recipe text are included.
