USDA RECIPE EXCERPTS
U.S. Department of Agriculture. Recipes for Healthy Kids: Cookbook for Homes.
Source URLs and page references accompany each excerpt.

"All are welcome to download these materials and make copies."
https://www.fna.usda.gov/tn/recipes-healthy-kids-cookbook-homes
