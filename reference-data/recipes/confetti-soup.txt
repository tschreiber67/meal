Confetti Soup

Preparation Time: 20 minutes
Cooking Time: 40 minutes
Makes six 1-cup servings

INGREDIENTS

1 3/4 tsp Canola oil
3/4 cup Fresh onions, peeled, diced
3/4 cup Fresh celery, diced
3/4 cup Fresh carrots, peeled, diced
1/2 tsp Salt
1/2 tsp Ground black pepper
1/4 tsp Whole fennel seed
1/8 tsp Crushed red pepper (optional)
1 1/2 cups Canned low-sodium black-eyed peas, drained, rinsed
3 1/2 cups Water
1 cup Extra-lean turkey ham, diced 1/4" (6 oz)
1/3 cup Fresh kale, coarsely chopped
1 1/2 Tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped

DIRECTIONS

1. In a large pot, heat oil over medium-high heat. Add onions and celery.
Cook for 2-3 minutes or until tender. Add carrots, salt, pepper, fennel
seed, and optional crushed red pepper. Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes.

2. Add black-eyed peas and water. Cook uncovered for 25 minutes over medium
heat.

3. Add turkey ham and kale. Cook covered for an additional 10 minutes over
medium heat until kale is tender.

4. Add parsley right before serving. Serve hot.
